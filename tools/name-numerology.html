Name & business name numerology

Naming a company, a product or a baby? Compare up to three options in one go.

Two systems, side by side

Pythagorean

Letters run 1 to 9 in alphabet order: A=1 … I=9, J=1 … R=9, S=1 … Z=8. Simple and the most common in Western books.

Chaldean

Letters are valued 1 to 8 by sound, and 9 is never assigned. Many Indian numerologists prefer it for names and brands, and read the unreduced "compound" total.

What the numbers mean

  • Expression — all letters; how a name presents to the world.
  • Soul urge — vowels only; inner motivation.
  • Personality — consonants only; first impressions.
  • Compound — the total before reducing; some schools read compounds 10–52 individually.
