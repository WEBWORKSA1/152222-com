FAQ

Does the score work for numeric domains?

Yes. The same memorability signals apply to domains like 888888.com or 152222.com. Domain value also depends on length, extension and buyer demand.

Is a higher score always better?

For recall, yes. For privacy, a very memorable number is also easier for strangers to guess and spam.
