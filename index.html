FAQ

Questions people ask

Is numerology scientific?

No. Numerology, angel numbers and lucky-number traditions are cultural belief systems, not science. We present them clearly labelled, alongside the verifiable maths of each number, so you can enjoy them and decide for yourself.

Do you store the numbers I enter?

No. All calculations run in your browser. Numbers are only sent to us if you choose to submit a form, such as a report request.

How is the VIP number score calculated?

It adds points for memorability signals — runs of the same digit, straight sequences, mirror patterns, repeated blocks, strong endings — and for using few distinct digits. It is a pattern score, not a price.

Can you get me a specific VIP mobile number?

Use the VIP number request. We pass qualified requests to vetted sellers and reply with what is available and the price. You buy directly and port or activate through your operator's process.

Is 152222.com connected to any company called 152222?

No. 152222 is simply a number and this site's domain. See our trademark disclosure.
