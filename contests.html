The puzzle

152222 has exactly 16 divisors. Find the smallest number greater than 152222 that also has exactly 16 divisors and whose digits add up to 14.

Hints: you can test any candidate in the number decoder — it lists divisors and digit sums. Show your working for a chance at a bonus prize for the clearest explanation.

How it works

  1. Solve the puzzle (tools allowed — it's about curiosity).
  2. Enter once per person before the countdown ends.
  3. After closing, we draw winners at random from correct entries and email them within 7 days.
  4. Answers and winners' first names are posted here the following month.
