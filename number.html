How the decoder works

Maths is exact for numbers up to 15 digits: factors come from trial division, divisors from the prime factorisation, and a number is abundant when its proper divisors add up to more than itself, deficient when they add up to less, and perfect when they match exactly (6, 28, 496…).

Numerology root adds the digits and keeps adding until one digit remains — the method practitioners use for phones, plates and houses. Every step is shown.

Pattern score rates how memorable the digits are: runs, sequences, mirrors and repeated blocks. It explains why some phone numbers and numeric domains sell at a premium.

Codes and dates show what the digits would mean as an Indian PIN, a US ZIP, a clock time or a date. These describe the numbering scheme — confirm specific places with the postal service.
